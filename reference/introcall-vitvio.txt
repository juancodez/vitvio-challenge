### About VitVio

### Design Process: Claro (End-to-End Project)

- Tax declaration platform for Spanish-speaking freelancers in Germany
  - Pain point identified from personal network: language barrier with German tax forms
  - Existing tools only in English, no Spanish-language alternative
- Research approach:
  - Macro: Perplexity + Google to size the market (Spanish-speaking freelancers in Germany)
  - Micro: 1 survey + 5 user interviews
  - Competitive benchmark: fed competitor tools into AI to inform product direction
- Shipped full product (front end + back end), not just a Figma prototype

### AI Workflow

- User interviews: Granola for transcription, then Claude for insight extraction
- Documentation: Google Notebook + Notion (connected to Claude)
- Prototyping: Claude Code as primary tool, with MCP/live connections as needed
  - Output can be HTML file, Figma, Lovable, or Figma Make depending on context
- Design-to-code philosophy: build components from Claude to Figma (not Figma to code)
  - Reduces handoff friction; engineer already has the code
  - Consistency assured by having design specs (padding, typography) defined upfront

### Collaboration and Disagreement Style

- Adapts vocabulary by audience: frames design decisions in business terms (retention, churn) not aesthetics
- Proactively shares ideas early to get feedback before investing effort
- On disagreement: only pushes back when there is no evidence or rationale behind a decision
  - Accepts direction he disagrees with if it is evidence-backed and explained
  - Example: deprioritized a feature he believed in after senior designer showed user feedback contradicting it
